IPv4 Service routing and resource metrics then worked. nftables IPv6 fib is unsupported in this kernel; the Pod/Service network is IPv4. Disabling kernel IPv6 was tested and reverted because control-plane clients use ::1. Kernel IPv6 remains enabled; IPv6 proxy warnings do not imply IPv4 failure and are retained in logs.
